# Dāginty ownership-transfer checklist

GitHub is the durable handoff location for company source files and non-sensitive assets. Platform ownership must still be transferred inside each service.

## ChatGPT Sites

- Confirm the company-controlled ChatGPT workspace and owner email.
- Give the company owner access to each existing Site when supported.
- If direct project ownership transfer is unavailable, create a new company-owned Site from the matching source archive.
- Reconnect custom domains and runtime services in the company workspace.
- Verify every live URL before removing Jay's access.

Jay's personal ChatGPT password and full chat history are not part of the transfer. Dāginty-funded use supported creation of the company sites; it does not require disclosure of unrelated private conversations, personal data or other client work.

## GitHub

- Repository: https://github.com/dagintyglobal/website
- Confirm at least two company administrators.
- Require multi-factor authentication for administrators.
- Keep passwords, tokens, recovery codes and private keys out of commits and issues.
- Record future site changes through pull requests or documented releases.

## Canva

- Michael currently has user access to Dāginty-related files in Jay's personal Canva account.
- Create or designate a company-controlled Canva account or team.
- Copy or transfer all Dāginty and Cluzy folders and designs to that account.
- Verify file ownership and editing access from the company account.
- Remove dependence on Jay's personal Canva account only after verification.
- Do not transfer Jay's full personal Canva account, which contains unrelated work.

## Substack

- Provide the company-controlled owner email.
- Transfer publication ownership through Substack.
- Confirm the company controls publication settings, subscriber administration, branding and payment settings, if used.
- Verify ownership before removing Jay's access.

## Domains and DNS

- Identify the registrar and DNS provider for every Dāginty and Cluzy domain.
- Add at least two company-controlled administrators.
- Move billing, recovery email and multi-factor authentication to company control.
- Record DNS entries before changing hosting.
- Test the replacement deployment before switching DNS.

## Other company assets

Place exportable, non-sensitive company materials in GitHub under a documented handoff folder or in company-controlled storage linked from the repository. This includes approved brand assets, website copy, public PDFs, press materials, sales collateral and technical documentation.

Do not store passwords, personal chat exports, subscriber data, customer data, legal records containing personal information or recovery codes in a public GitHub repository.

## Completion record

For every platform, record:

- Current owner
- New company owner
- Company administrator
- Transfer date
- Verification date
- Final URL
- Remaining dependency on Jay

The transfer is complete only when a company administrator can sign in, edit, publish and recover the account without Jay's credentials.
